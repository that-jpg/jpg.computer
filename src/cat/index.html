Start quietly. Keep your device away from your cat’s ears.

How to use it

First, click and immediately give a small treat. Repeat until your cat connects the sound with a reward.

Then click the instant your cat does something you want to encourage, and follow with a treat. Keep sessions short and stop if your cat seems uncomfortable.

No sound? Check your device’s media volume and silent mode. With the button focused, you can also press Space or Enter.
